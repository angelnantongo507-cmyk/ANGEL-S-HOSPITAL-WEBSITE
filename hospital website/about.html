About Us

Who we are and what drives us

Our Story

LifeCare Hospital & Medical Centre began as a small clinic in the heart of Kampala, founded on the belief that every person deserves access to quality, dignified healthcare — regardless of their circumstances.

Today, we serve thousands of patients every month, and our commitment to medical excellence and compassionate care has never wavered.

Who We Are

LifeCare Hospital & Medical Centre is a full-service healthcare facility where patient well-being comes first — from the moment you walk through our doors to your complete recovery.

Every treatment plan is carefully developed by our medical team to ensure that what reaches you is nothing short of the highest standard of care.

Our departments include General Medicine, Paediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Orthopaedics, Dentistry and Surgery.

Our diagnostic services include laboratory tests, X-ray, ultrasound, and pharmacy services.
